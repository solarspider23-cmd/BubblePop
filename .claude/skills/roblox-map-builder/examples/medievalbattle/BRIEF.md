# Riverfield Siege (brief)

**Game:** team PvP medieval battle (2 teams, 8-12 a side): capture-the-bridge / king-of-the-hill rounds. Each team
spawns in its own keep; the contested ground is a river valley between them with three crossings.

**Archetype:** mirrored **Arena** (new): one bounded valley, 640 x 400 studs, mirror-symmetric about x = 0 (fair
spawns). Two keeps on raised plateaus at x = -255 (Crimson) and x = +255 (Azure); a north-south river at x = 0 splits
the field. Three lanes cross it: the Sundered Bridge (centre), Chapel Isle (north, two timber footbridges onto a
rocky islet) and the Southern Ford (shallow water, wadeable, trench lines on both banks).

- **Spawn:** Crimson courtyard at (-238, 15, -10), facing east (+X) through the open gatehouse: the Sundered Bridge
  tower closes the view 240 studs away, framed by the gate arch. Azure mirrors it.
- **Hero:** the Sundered Bridge tower: a square stone gate tower on the central pier of a three-arch bridge, open
  belfry with a bronze bell, broken slate spire; one arch collapsed into the river and bridged by a timber patch.
- **Theme pushed into every prop:** a war that has gone on for a season: pale limestone ashlar with mortar joints,
  dark oak with iron straps and rivets, rope lashings, straw, scorched ends. Team colour only on painted shields,
  shutters, tent canvas and stripes on wood (crimson / azure). **No flags, banners or pennants.**
- **Signature edge detail:** the sharpened-stake palisade (logs with cut points, a rope lashing band and an iron
  cramp) on every trench lip, camp edge and riverbank at the ford.
- **Shared base:** ashlar plinth with a chamfered top course (towers, bridge piers, chapel, well).
- **Look:** storm-break late afternoon: slate-blue overcast sky, a low warm amber sun (22 deg) breaking through from
  the south-west, long shadows, a thin warm battle-smoke haze. Glow only from fire (braziers, campfires, torches) and
  chapel candles.
- **Not this:** studs / Parts, voxel blocks, hub bowl, plots, zone strip, islands, crater rings, candy colours, flags
  / banners / pennants, text in meshes, ground-carved detail that pixelates (scorch craters are modelled props).
