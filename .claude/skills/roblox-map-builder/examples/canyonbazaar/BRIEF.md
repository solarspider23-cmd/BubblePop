# Sunspire Canyon Bazaar (brief)

**Game:** simulator / adventure hub: spawn, take quests, sell loot at the trader, upgrade at the forge, buy pets /
relics up on the ledge, walk the canyon to the temple at the end (rebirth / teleport to the next world).

**Archetype:** **linear canyon run** (new): ONE meandering canyon floor walked from south (spawn) to north (temple),
~100 wide x 510 long, framed by stepped red-rock mesa walls (risers to y 24 / ~52 / ~86) whose first shelf is a
walkable ledge on both sides. No tiers, no ring, no plots: a single readable line with a vertical side loop.

- **Spawn:** star medallion (0, 0, 226) facing -Z: the Market Gate frames the street, the temple sits in the arch.
- **Hero:** Temple of the Setting Sun: Petra-style two-storey rock-cut facade (fluted columns, frieze, broken
  pediment, tholos with a sun urn), glowing doorway + sun disc, standing in a rectangular niche CUT into the sheer end
  wall (three-step chiselled surround, cliff strata continuing round it), placed at 1.35 scale (~95 studs tall).
- **Traversal:** walk the floor; side loop = east carved stair (oasis) -> east ledge -> Relic Kiosk -> rope bridge ->
  west ledge -> west carved stair down to the narrows.
- **Palette:** warm base = sand floor, cream sandstone ashlar / paving, red + orange cliff strata, terracotta pots,
  dark palm timber. Accents ONLY on cloths + goods: teal, saffron, plum; turquoise water; warm brass lanterns.
- **Signature edge detail:** a sandstone parapet module with a cut arched niche holding a glowing brass lantern, along
  the plaza, oasis and court wall feet and the lip of both ledges. Shared base: two-course sandstone dais.
- **Look:** crisp dusk: warm gold key 12 deg from the south-south-west up the canyon onto the temple, cool blue-violet
  fill in the shadows, gold horizon -> blue-violet top, very light haze, pale warm sand vs deep red / orange / cream
  strata, saturated teal + plum cloth and turquoise water, lanterns and braziers glowing.
- **Not this:** flags, banners, pennants (cloth only stretched flat over frames), text in meshes, tiers / rings /
  radial layout, water-first, studs.
