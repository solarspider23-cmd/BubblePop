# Whispering Grove — forest RNG quest map (brief)

- Fantasy: cosy enchanted forest clearing where you roll for blades/auras and take quests. Style: flat stylised
  (palette-strip gradients, faceted modelled foliage, no photo textures). 1 unit = 1 stud, Roblox space (X east, Y up).
- Size: 360 x 360 studs. Playable clearing r ~115; forest wall + rising hills r 115-180 contain it.
- Spawn: south edge of the plaza (0, 0, 22) facing -Z at the hero.
- Hero: Elder Tree, 80+ studs, at (0, -130) on a mound, glowing door in its trunk facing the plaza.
- Centre: RNG Altar on a voronoi-paved plaza (r 28): 3-tier octagon dais, floating crystal + rune rings, 4 rune stones.
- Districts: west Quest Camp (board with "!" marker, tents, campfire, crates) | east Glowshroom Grove (mushroom ring
  round a crystal cluster) | north stream + arched bridge on the path to the Elder Tree | south entrance road with lanterns.
- Paths: dirt, 16 wide main, 12 side; fences + lanterns at nodes; story groups at bends.
- Palette: bark/leaf/pine/grass/moss/dirt/stone/wood + accents gold (rewards, altar), cyan (magic), purple (crystals),
  red (mushrooms). Saturation on hero, altar and interactables, never on the ground.
- Not this: flags/banners, photo textures, alpha leaf cards, straight-line paths, evenly sprinkled props.
