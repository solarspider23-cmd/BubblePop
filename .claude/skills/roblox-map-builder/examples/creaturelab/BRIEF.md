# Bloomcrest Care Centre (brief)

**Game:** creature-collecting game hub: heal your creatures, buy capture capsules / potions / berries, manage storage on
PC terminals, register for trades and battles, and pick a starter in the research lab. Original identity in the genre's
friendly, bright, clean style. No real-franchise designs: no red/white ball, no red "P" roof, no real creatures, no text.

**Archetype:** **interior-first hub** (new): a walkable building interior with a small exterior entry plaza. One round
care hall (rotunda, inner r 44, walls 22, ceiling dome to ~31 with a glass oculus) and a research wing (48 wide, 80 deep,
half-round end) through an 18 wide arch on the north axis. Stations stand round the hall wall so the centre stays an open
plaza; the wing is one long room with the hero at its far end on the same axis as the entrance.

- **Spawn:** plaza (0, 0.35, 118) facing -Z: path, entrance, arch and the Bloom Chamber line up on one axis.
- **Hero:** outside, the round hall with its mint dome, glass oculus and the canopy badge; inside, the Bloom Chamber at
  the end of the axis (glowing rings, most saturated + brightest mass).
- **Identity:** the Bloom Paw (rounded pad + three leaf toes, a shape) and the Gem Pod capsule (faceted 8-sided pod:
  white lower shell, tier-coloured upper shell, steel ring latch with a glowing gem stud, hinge block). Tiers: mint, sky,
  coral, gold.
- **Palette:** clean white walls, soft cream counters and tiles, brushed steel, light birch, lawn green. Accents: mint +
  teal (care, trim, roof), coral (mart), sky (storage, trade), gold (premium). Glow: heal mint, liquid green / pink,
  screens pale blue, chamber cyan, lamps warm white.
- **Scale:** avatars ~5.5. Doors 14 wide, arch 18 wide, aisles >= 10, counters 3.6 high, ceiling 22 at the wall.
- **Look:** bright clean late morning, sun 48 deg from the south-east, pale cyan horizon to blue zenith, no haze; the
  interior is evenly lit (Roblox: Ambient + lamp PointLights).
- **Not this:** red/white ball, red roof + letter emblem, real creatures, text / digits in geometry (names = GUIs on
  anchors), flags / banners, studs, dark or moody interiors, tall walls that trap the camera (roofs are separate props).
