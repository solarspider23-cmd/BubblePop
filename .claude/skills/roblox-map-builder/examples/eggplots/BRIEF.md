# Eggstead Isle (brief)

**Game:** "Steal a ___" egg game (collect, upgrade, steal). Eggs ride a conveyor down the central lane; a player buys
one and carries it home to an incubator nest in their plot; others raid the plots past the scripted laser gates.
Original egg / spring-farm identity, low poly, flat-shaded, chunky, bright. No text, letters, digits, flags or banners.

**Archetype:** facing plot rows across a conveyor lane (requested layout, tycoon-plot family) on an island arena
in a lake. 8 identical plots (two rows of 4). One plot-local kit is placed 8 times, rotated about the lane axis for fairness.

- **Spawn:** (0, 0.75, 178) facing north: drain (kept low), belt and the hatchery on one axis.
- **Accents:** west 1-4 red, yellow, sky, purple; east 5-8 orange, teal, blue, pink. Neutral base: straw yards,
  cream plaster, honey wood, warm stone; the accent is on roofs, fence rails, gates, nest rings, pads, signs, mats.
- **Look:** crisp spring morning, sun 34 deg from the east-south-east, warm white key, pale mint horizon to clear
  blue, very thin cool haze.
- **Not this:** text in geometry, flags / banners, primitive-box props, layouts or art copied from existing games.
