# Moon Sweepers (brief)

**Game:** clean-the-Moon simulator. Players vacuum moon dust, sweep regolith patches and haul space junk, sell the haul
at the Dust Exchange, buy bigger vacuums / tanks at the Gear Locker, hatch alien eggs, rebirth in the Re-Entry Pod and
buy their way DOWN into a giant crater, ring by ring.

**Archetype:** terraced crater descent (new): one huge complex crater (r ~270) with concentric shelves stepping down to a
central peak. Hub on the north rim plateau OUTSIDE the crater, looking in and down. Each shelf ring = one zone; a ramp
cut into the riser between two shelves, gated by a priced **airlock arch** at its head.

- **Spawn:** hub landing pad at (0, 8, -318), facing south (+Z) through the open Gate 1 airlock into the crater: the
  rocket on the central peak closes the view, framed by the airlock, with Earth in the black sky beside it.
- **Hero / landmark:** the Moon Rocket (white/orange, gold-foil collar, fins, gantry tower with arms) on the central
  peak - visible from every shelf. Hub landmark: a gold-foil Lunar Lander beside spawn.
- **Theme pushed into every prop:** retro space programme: white panels, gold foil, orange safety, chevrons, rivets,
  round port windows, antennae.
- **Signature edge detail:** runway beacon rail on every shelf lip and pad edge: low white posts with an amber glow cap
  and a thin cable between them (in zone colour on the gates).
- **Shared station base:** a hexagonal landing pad on four splayed lander legs with an orange chevron rim.
- **Stations (hub):** Dust Exchange (sell: hopper + vacuum intake + canister rack), Gear Locker (shop: vacuum packs on
  a rack + hologram vacuum gun), Re-Entry Pod (rebirth: capsule + glow ring), Egg Incubator (pets: cryo domes with eggs).
- **Look:** lunar day: black sky with stars, hard white low sun (18 deg) from the east, long black shadows, no haze,
  big blue Earth in the sky. Neon accents: cyan, amber, mint (sell), magenta (eggs).
- **Not this:** studs/Parts, bowl with radial paths, islands, plots, straight strip, colourful candy palette, haze,
  flags/banners, text in meshes.
