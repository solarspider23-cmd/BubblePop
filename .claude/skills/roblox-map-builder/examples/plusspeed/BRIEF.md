# +1 Speed Run (brief)

**Game:** "+1 Speed every second" runner. Train on treadmills in the hub, run the straight track as far as you can,
collect wins pads, unlock the next zone gate, hatch pets, rebirth. Classic +1 simulator genre and stud look.
**Archetype:** linear zone strip (map_archetypes.md: Open zone strip / linear course).
**Style:** low-poly stud. Everything is Parts (Block / Wedge / Cylinder / Ball) on Glacier + stud MaterialVariant,
Neon for glow. No meshes. Exported as an .rbxmx of Parts.

- Hub (z +110..-100): grass plaza, checker path, spawn pad facing the track, 6 treadmills (training tiers), egg
  hatchery, shop stall, rebirth altar, leaderboard, voxel trees + lamps, START arch over the track mouth.
- Track: 144 wide (3 lanes of 48 x 50 checker tiles), 8 zones x 250 studs along -Z:
  1 Meadow, 2 Desert, 3 Beach, 4 Candy, 5 Snow, 6 Volcano, 7 Neon City, 8 Space. Each zone: its own two-tone
  checker, stepped canyon walls in zone colours (2 tiers), themed voxel dressing on the ledges, lamp pillars, a zone
  gate (pillars + banner board + veil) at its start, wins pads at its end, distance boards every 50.
- Finish (z -2100..-2260): finish arch, podium with a giant blocky trophy, the big wins pad.
- Look: bright cartoon midday, blue sky, blocky clouds.

## v4 concept: an original build that applies general zone-strip lessons, not any existing layout
- Theme everywhere is SPEED / RACING: sneakers, stopwatches, lightning bolts, race kerbs, medals, a running track.
- Signature edge detail: a red/white race kerb lip with wedge bevels on every cliff tier, in zone colours.
- Landmark that closes the forward view: the START gate, crowned by a giant pixel stopwatch (white face, glowing tick
  marks, red hands, crown button).
- Hub floor: a stadium. An oval running track with white lanes rings a striped infield. Spawn is on the infield,
  facing START.
- Shared station base: a white chamfered plinth with a kerb trim and a glowing lightning-bolt inlay at the front.
- Hero props are pixel-art silhouettes (stepped blocks, outline rim standing proud, glowing accents):
  - Shop: a giant sneaker.
  - Hatchery: a giant cracked egg.
  - Rebirth: a bolt portal ring with gem studs.
  - Wins pads: a medal with ribbons.
  - Signs: pixel letters.
