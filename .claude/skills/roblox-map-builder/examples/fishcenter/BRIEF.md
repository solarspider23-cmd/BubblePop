# Tidehook Lagoon (brief)

**Game:** fishing-centre simulator hub (fishing-sim genre): spawn, buy a rod and bait, walk out to a pier end
and fish, sell the catch at the market, check the aquarium index, take quests, rent a boat for the open water.

**Archetype:** **radial ring lagoon** (new): a FLAT stilt resort on open water. One ring boardwalk (inner r 58, outer
r 70, deck y 4.5, water y 0) round a round lagoon; four spoke bridges cross the lagoon to a central platform with the
hero; stations sit on their own deck pads OUTSIDE the ring; three fishing piers and a boat dock reach out into deep water;
a broken reef ring of sand islets with palms (r ~210-270) frames the fishing water; open sea and volcanic islands beyond.

| Bearing (from the centre) | What is there |
|---|---|
| S (spawn) | Spawn Plaza pad (44 x 31) + driftwood Welcome Arch on the axis, Quest Board, benches, torches |
| SW | Rod Shop (red awning, rod racks, brass fish-hook icon on the ridge) |
| SE | Bait Shop (round bamboo hut, lime awning, lure icon, bait barrels + cooler) |
| E | Fish Market / sell counter (open pavilion, ice counter with fish, scale, marlin icon) |
| W | Boat Dock (teal boathouse, main pier with two slips, a skiff + a motor fishing boat) |
| NE / N / NW | Fishing piers to r 125-150 with end platforms; railing gaps at the ends = fishing spots, marker buoys |
| centre | Lighthouse Aquarium (hero): glass dome aquarium (fish + coral inside) with a red/white lighthouse rising out of it; Fish Index wall by the door |

- **Spawn:** plaza (0, 4.5, 88) facing -Z: arch, ring gap, south spoke, dome door and lighthouse line up on one axis.
- **Hero:** Lighthouse Aquarium, lamp at ~y 66, the only tall thing; red stripes = the most saturated mass.
- **Frame:** outer reef islets (sand + palms + coral rocks) with gaps for boats; sea stacks; distant volcanic islands
  and cumulus at the horizon.
- **Palette:** silver-tan weathered deck, dark teak posts, honey bamboo, golden thatch, white sand, pale coral stone,
  palm green, turquoise water. Saturated ONLY on 4 station accents: coral red (rod shop, lighthouse, lifebuoys), sun
  yellow (market, buoys), lagoon teal (boathouse, glass floats), lime (bait shop).
- **Signature edge detail:** teak rail posts capped with teal glass fishing floats in a rope net, double rope swags,
  a striped float hanging every third bay; tiki torches at every gap. Shared base: plank deck on teak pilings.
- **Look:** bright tropical late morning: sun 40 deg from the east-south-east, warm white, cyan horizon > azure zenith,
  very thin aqua haze, turquoise water over white sand, deep blue beyond the reef.
- **Not this:** tiers / stairs / terraces, mountains, golden hour, flags / banners / pennants, text or digits in
  geometry (names = GUIs on invisible anchors), studs, even scatter.
