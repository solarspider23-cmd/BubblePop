# Frostpeak — snowy mountain village RNG hub (brief)

- Fantasy: a cosy snowed-in village in a mountain bowl where players roll at a frozen shrine. Same flat stylised look
  as Whispering Grove (palette strips, faceted shapes), reusing its kit where it fits (rocks, lanterns, fences, crates).
- Size ~320 x 320 studs; bowl rim rises into cliffs + snowy pines.
- Spawn: south gate (0, 0, 70) facing -Z.
- Hero: Frost Spire — a 60-stud ice crystal shrine on a stone terrace at the north end, visible over the rooftops.
- Centre: village square with the roll well (stone well, rope, glowing ice core) and market stalls.
- Districts: cabins ring the square (6-8, lit windows, smoking chimneys implied by caps), west sled run slope,
  east frozen pond with ice-fishing huts, north stair up to the spire terrace.
- Palette: snow white-blue ground, dark pine, warm wood + lit windows (warm accent), ice cyan (magic accent).
  Warm lights carry the cosy read; ice glow only on the hero, well core and pond.
- Not this: flags/banners, noisy textures, floating props, flat plates pasted on walls (doors/windows are built in).
