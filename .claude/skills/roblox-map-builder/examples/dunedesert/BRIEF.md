# The Gilded Dunes (brief)

**Game:** open-world treasure / adventure sim: spawn on the sun dais, take quests, buy at the caravan trader, upgrade at
the sun astrolabe, roam the dune sea between six points of interest, dig treasure mounds (tiers 1-3, richer further
out), enter the sunken temple dungeon.

**Archetype:** OPEN-WORLD RADIAL DUNE SEA (new): a sandstone ruin city with a step pyramid in the centre, POIs on
spokes 180-250 studs out, sand paths with waystones between them, a ring of high dune ridges (r ~350-470) as the frame,
tiered mesas far beyond. ~730 x 730 playable.

- **Spawn:** (0, 1.6, 200) facing -Z: the pylon gate frames the pyramid up the avenue.
- **Hero:** six-tier gilded step pyramid (100 x 100, ~74 tall) with a front stair and a glowing sun shrine on top.
- **Traversal:** open walking over the dunes in any direction; paths are a guide, not a corridor; climb the pyramid
  stair; walk down into the temple trench; walk under the rib arches.
- **Palette:** pale gold sand, gilded cream sandstone, rose sandstone for the poorer ruin walls, gold caps; accents
  only on nomad cloth (indigo, madder red, saffron), oasis turquoise, palm green; rust mesas far away.
- **Signature detail:** raised sun-disc relief (disc + ray wedges) on every built piece; shared base = stepped plinth
  with a gold trim line.
- **Look:** high midday sun (64 deg), short crisp shadows, deep blue zenith fading to a bleached horizon, thin warm
  heat haze that softens the far ridges and mesas.
- **Not this:** canyon / corridor, dusk, flags or banners, glyphs or letters on reliefs, tiers, rings of stations.
