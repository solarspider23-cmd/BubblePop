# Sugarcrest Kingdom (brief)

**Game:** candy-world simulator hub. Players spawn at the cupcake fountain, walk the board-game trail between
stations: sweet shop, bakery upgrade oven, egg machine for pets, quest stand and a gumball-machine teleport. They can
also cross the chocolate river to the candy castle.
**Archetype:** BOARD-GAME TRAIL (new). There are three tile routes, each a run of coloured candy squares with "stops"
(round tile plazas) at every station:
- one straight Royal Road from the spawn to the castle;
- a west loop and an east loop that wind round the kingdom.

A chocolate river from Fudge Falls cuts across all three routes, so each one crosses it a different way: a waffle
bridge, a biscuit bridge or marshmallow stepping stones.

| Where | What |

| Castle hill (0, -180) | plateau y 6 inside a blue-raspberry jelly moat with biscuit walls, drawbridge on candy posts, gingerbread castle with 4 ice-cream-cone towers and a soft-serve keep |
| Forecourt (0, -92) | lemon stop plaza, quest stand |
| Fudge Falls (-262, -104) | grape gumdrop mountain, chocolate spring pouring from a cave mouth into a cream-froth pool: the head of the river |
| Frame | ring of 24 sugar-coated gumdrop mountains with dripping icing caps; cotton-candy cloud ring beyond |

- **Terrain:** mint frosting ground with rolling hills. Most hills wear a poured icing cap (vanilla / strawberry /
  chocolate) with a scalloped edge and round-tipped drips. The river bed has graham-crumb banks.
- **Palette:** mint frosting, vanilla icing, gingerbread and chocolate as the base. The accents are hard-candy cherry,
  berry, lime, lemon, grape, blue and orange, with blue-raspberry jelly. The board tiles cycle six candy colours.
- **Look:** soft pastel late morning, sun 38 deg from the south-east (it lights the castle front). The sky runs peach >
  pink > lilac > blue, with thin pink haze.
- **Not this:** plot grid (Sugarworks), islands in a lagoon (Puffpaw Isles), faceted hull props, text or flags.
